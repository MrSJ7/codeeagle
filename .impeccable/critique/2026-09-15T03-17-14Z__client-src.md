#### Design Specificity Verdict

#### Overall Impression
CodeEagle has exceptional underlying engineering—deterministic AST safety, cryptographic SHA-256 patch validation, and automated post-patch re-analysis—trapped inside an unconvincing, fragmented interface. Transforming it from an AI dashboard template into an authoritative developer instrument requires replacing card grids with integrated editorial product showcases, harmonizing the dark/light palette into a cohesive atmosphere, connecting findings directly to inline PR-style comments, and streamlining the patch workflow.

#### What's Working
1. **Uncompromising Stale-Source Guardrails**: Instant invalidation of review state on code mutation with orange warning states prevents unsafe mutations.
2. **Deterministic-to-Semantic Hybrid Provenance**: Clear attribution of findings to AST rules vs. AI reasoning builds developer confidence.
3. **Traceable Review History**: Seamless side-by-side audit history tracking pre- and post-patch score deltas.

- **[P0] Disconnected Patch Workflow & Modal Disruption**: Static issues patch blindly without preview; AI issues open a viewport-blocking modal. *Fix*: Unify into an inline PR-style suggested diff comment with side-by-side/unified preview and single-click execution.
